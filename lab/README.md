# lab: not shipped

Work kept for later, outside the product. Nothing here is in the HUD, the Read Me or the listing, and CI does not
check it.

- **`frame-full-4.5.0/`**: the full 4.5.0 "Photographer" mode, with four subjects (you, a picked person, a group, the
  landscape), five framings each, photo shapes 16:9 / 4:3 / 3:2, a lens line in mm, and its own menus and person picker.
  Take 12 ships a slim version of it as Frame (`hud/src/cinema_frame.lsl`). This is the starting point for a
  possible later, more serious composition tool. `photographer_sim.py` there still runs
  (`python3 lab/frame-full-4.5.0/photographer_sim.py`) and checks its own script.
- **`sinking-boat/`**: a gag boat (not Take 12): it bobs and drifts on open water, and slowly sinks with whoever sits
  in it. `sinking_boat.lsl`, set-up notes in its README.
