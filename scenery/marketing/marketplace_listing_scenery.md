## Full description
```
See your Second Life from somewhere other than behind your own back. Free.

Take 12 Scenery lifts your camera off your avatar and places it where a camera operator would: high above the region, out across the horizon, or in a corner of your skybox. It chooses each view by itself, holds it for about a minute, then cuts to the next. A double-tap starts it or skips ahead, a tap stops it, and any movement key gives your camera back at once.

THE SCENERY CAMERA
- Drone views looking down over your area, and wide views along the horizon, up to 100 m away, always with a clear view.
- In a skybox, a sky club or a sky mall it stays inside, with views from the corners and of you, small in the space.
- Idle start: stay idle for 3 minutes (adjustable, or off) and it starts by itself. Come back to a slow film of the place you are in.

ONE BUTTON
- Double-tap: start, or the next view.
- Tap: the menu, always (while the camera runs: Photo, Next, Stop).
- Any movement key: your camera, instantly.

ALSO
- Photo hold: freeze the view while you take snapshots.
- Pace: 30 s to 2 min per view.
- A quiet line above the button says when the camera runs, with your lens; nothing there means it is your own camera.
- Short tips while you are new to it; they turn themselves off.
- One small pixel-art clapper in sepia, with a red recording light and a tiny clap now and then while the camera runs.

WANT MORE CAMERAS?
Take 12 Scenery is the scenery camera of Take 12. The full Take 12 adds Me (shots of you, following you), Us (you and the people around you, or only the friends you pick, in one frame), Still (a held picture of you, with the classic portrait lens), your lens in every camera, and Connect: connect with friends, and everyone sees the camera of whoever films. Tap the button > More cameras.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Wear it on a HUD point (Top Right is usual) and allow camera control once.
- It only moves your view, inside your region. Nothing is recorded.
- Read Me notecard included.
- Free to keep and to give. Permissions: (you)
- Support: (you)
```
