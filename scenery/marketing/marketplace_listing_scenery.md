## Full description
```
Free. The place, while you are away.

Take 12 Scenery lifts your camera off your avatar and shows the place you are in: high above the region, out across the horizon, or from a corner of your skybox. It chooses each view by itself and holds it for about a minute. Stay idle for a few minutes and it starts on its own.

- Double-tap: start, or the next view. Tap: the menu. Any movement key: your camera back.
- In a skybox or a sky club it stays inside, on your own floor.
- It only moves your view, inside your region. Nothing is recorded.

The scenery camera of Take 12. The full Take 12 adds Framing (you and your friends, composed), Freehand (you frame it, it holds it) and Connect (one camera for everyone near you): tap the button > More cameras.

Free to keep and to give. Permissions: (you)  ·  Support: (you)
```
