Take 12 Scenery  -  READ ME
Free. The scenery camera of Take 12, on its own.

Take 12 Scenery moves your camera off your avatar to the places a camera operator would choose: high drone views over the area, wide views across the horizon, and the corners of your skybox. It changes the view by itself about once a minute. Any movement key brings your normal camera back. It only moves your view: nothing is recorded.


=== QUICK START =========================================

1. Wear the HUD. Right-click it > Attach To HUD > Top Right.
2. Accept the camera permission when asked. (It is needed to move your view. It is only asked once.)
3. DOUBLE-TAP the small clapper button.

That is it. Your camera lifts off and starts showing you the place. Press any movement key, or tap the button once, and your own camera is right back.

Or simply stay idle: after 3 minutes with no keys and no mouse (adjustable in the menu) the scenery camera starts by itself. See IDLE START below for the mouse check.


=== THE BUTTON ==========================================

The small clapper is the whole HUD. Everything is done with taps.

TAP
   - when idle: opens the menu
   - while rolling: stops (your camera is back)
   - while frozen (Photo): carries on

DOUBLE-TAP  (two quick taps)
   - when idle: starts the scenery camera
   - while rolling or frozen: cuts to the next view, now

HOLD  (about 1 second)
   - while rolling: the live menu (Photo, Next, Stop)
   - when idle: the menu

While the camera runs, the clapper gives a tiny clap now and then; with your own camera it is still. (Its look: Options > Look.)


=== THE SCENERY CAMERA ==================================

It looks at the place, not at people. High drone views looking down over the area, and wide views along the horizon, up to about 100 m away, always with a clear view.

On a build in the sky (a skybox, a sky mall, a sky club), it stays INSIDE: views from up high, at eye level, and you as a small figure in the space, always on your own floor. On the ground in a house, it looks from outside, above the roof (Options > Camera > Indoors > Inside keeps it inside there too).


=== ABOVE THE BUTTON ====================================

While the camera runs, a short line above the button says so:

   ◇ Scenery · auto · 21 mm ✓                you started it
   ◇ Scenery · started while you were idle   the idle start began it
   ▣ Photo · held                            tap to carry on
   ◇ Scenery · paused · Esc to carry on      you moved the camera yourself

Nothing above the button means: it is your own camera.
The number is your lens: your viewer's zoom (Ctrl+0 in, Ctrl+8 out, Ctrl+9 back to normal). Scenery likes it wide, up to 28 mm; zooming in gives a telephoto detail of the same view.
Options > Look > Status line turns the line off.

While you are new to it, a short tip shows there now and then. Each shows only a few times, then Tips turns itself off. Help > Tips turns them on or off.


=== THE LIVE MENU (hold the button while it rolls) ======

PHOTO     Freeze the view so you can take your pictures in peace. Tap once to carry on.
NEXT      Cut to the next view now.
STOP      Give your camera back.


=== SETTINGS (tap the button > Options) =================

TIMING    PACE: how long each view is held: Lively 30 s, Easy 45 s, Calm 60 s (default), Slow 90 s, Slowest 120 s.
          IDLE START: when the scenery camera starts by itself: off, 1 min, 90 s, 2 min, 3 min (default), 5 min or 10 min with no keys, no typing and no camera moves.
          MOUSE CHECK (on by default): it also waits until your viewer marks you Away. A script cannot see the mouse, but the viewer can. Set your viewer's own "Away" timer short (in its Preferences, e.g. 2 or 3 minutes), or the scenery camera waits for it. If your viewer never sets you Away, turn the mouse check off.
CAMERA    INDOORS: Auto (default): inside on a build in the sky, or under a roof; outdoor views on open land. Inside: always inside, near you. Outside: always outdoor views.
          REACH: how far the camera may go (50 / 100 / 150 / 200 m; 100 by default). If your viewer holds the camera closer, it lowers itself; Re-learn goes back to your choice.
          ALT-CAM: what happens if you Alt-orbit your camera: pause (default), end, or ignore.
LOOK      Menu style (Symbols, Plain or Color), the button (Classic, Rec or Viewfinder), the status line and chat text.
TEST CAM  A 3-second check that the HUD can move your camera.
RESET     Back to factory settings.


=== MORE CAMERAS ========================================

Take 12 Scenery is the scenery camera of Take 12. The full Take 12 adds:
 - ME: composed shots of you that follow you as you walk.
 - US: you and the people around you, cutting to whoever speaks; or only the friends you pick, all in one frame.
 - STILL: a held picture of you, with the classic portrait lens for each framing.
 - SHARE THE VIEW: go live, and your friends watch your camera.
 - Your lens in every camera, favourite spots, and the seated view from above.
Tap the button > More cameras to see it on the Marketplace.


=== QUESTIONS ===========================================

Nothing happens when I double-tap.
Tap once and use the menu to start Scenery. If that works, tap twice a little quicker, about as fast as a double-click.

The menu buttons show boxes.
Options > Look > Plain (words only).

The camera stays close to me.
Some viewers limit how far a script may place the camera. The HUD notices and shortens its distance by itself. If it ever feels too short, Options > Camera > Reach > Re-learn.

It does not stay inside my skybox.
Some builds are made without physics, and the HUD cannot see them. Set Options > Camera > Indoors > Inside: it then always stays inside, near you.

Does it work in a region I do not own?
Yes. The camera stays inside the region you are standing in.


=== GOOD TO KNOW ========================================

 - Wear it on a HUD point (Top Right is the usual one).
 - Take it off any time; your own camera is always restored.
 - Free to keep and to give to friends.

Enjoy the view.
