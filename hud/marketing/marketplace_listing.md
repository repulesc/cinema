## Full description
```
See your Second Life from somewhere other than behind your own back, and let your friends watch it with you.

Take 12 lifts your camera off your avatar and places it where a camera operator would: high above the region, out across the horizon, in a corner of your skybox, or around you and your friends. It chooses each view by itself, holds it for about a minute, then cuts to the next one.

You stay in control. A double-tap on the button starts it or skips to the next view, a single tap stops it, and any movement key gives your normal camera back instantly.

FOUR CAMERAS

Scenery (the default) shows the place itself. Drone views 10 to 30 m up look down over your area, and wide eye-level views run along the horizon, up to 100 m away. Every spot is checked so that nothing blocks the picture. You are a small figure in the frame, or not in it at all. In a skybox or another closed room in the sky, it stays inside: views from the corners, from the middle of the room, and of you, small in the space. In a house on the ground, it looks from outside, above the roof. Scenery starts with a double-tap, or by itself when you are idle.

Me shows you. Composed shots from different sides, with room in front of you in the direction you face. When you walk, it re-frames.

Us is for when you are with people. Choose Everyone near: over-the-shoulder shots between you and the person nearest to you, group shots of everyone within 8 m, and a cut to whoever speaks in local chat. Or choose Only friends: pick up to 10 friends from the people near you, and the camera holds you and them in one frame, glides slowly as the group moves, and widens when someone wanders toward the edge.

Still is for a picture of yourself, or of you and a friend. It holds one well-composed view: full length, half length or close. Double-tap for the next, use your movement keys to adjust (Shift + A/D slides you across the picture), take your snapshot. A lens guide names the portrait lens for each framing (50, 85 or 105 mm) and shows yours live: zoom your viewer to it, and the camera backs off for that flattering telephoto look.

Me, Us and Still are chosen from the menu. Going idle only ever starts Scenery.

FRAME IT YOURSELF
In Only friends, set the view up the way you like it with your own camera: closer, higher, from the side. Press Esc, and Take 12 carries on with your view, just as close, as high and aimed at the same faces, following your friends as they move. When you sit or lie down, the camera looks down on you from above.

SHARE THE VIEW
Hold the button and choose Go live. Everyone near you who wears Take 12 or the free Take 12 Watch follows your camera, at once, with nothing to click. The Watch is a tiny free HUD that you can hand to any friend.
- Above your button you always see who is connected: "LIVE - 3 watching" and their names.
- Double-tap while watching, and everyone gets the next view.
- Look around with your own camera, and Esc brings you back to the shared view.
- Pass the camera to a friend, or let anyone ask to lead. You decide: ask me each time, always, or never.
- It stays inside the region, and ends for everyone when you stop.

YOUR LENS
Your viewer's zoom is your lens, and Take 12 frames for it in every camera. Zoom in, and the camera glides back so everyone keeps their size: the flatter, natural faces of a portrait lens. A longer lens also brings more close views of faces, a wider one more of the place. The line above the button shows your lens and the range each camera likes, and Still names the exact portrait lens for each framing.

ALWAYS KNOW WHAT IS ON
- A quiet line above the button says which camera runs and who is in charge: "Scenery - auto", "Only friends - your view", "Photo - held". Nothing above the button means it is your own camera.
- Short tips show up while you are new to it, and turn themselves off.
- Menus in quiet symbols, plain words or colour: your choice.

ALSO
- Photo hold: freeze the current view while you take snapshots.
- Favourite spots: save a view you like, and it comes back now and then.
- Pace: from 30 seconds to 2 minutes per view.
- Idle start after 3 minutes with no keys and no mouse (adjustable, or off).
- Typing in chat does not stop Us.

CONTROLS
- Tap: open the menu. While the camera runs, a tap stops it.
- Double-tap: start Scenery, or skip to the next view.
- Hold: share your view.
- Any movement key: your normal camera, instantly.

WHO IT IS FOR
- Explorers who want to see a region from above and from afar.
- Photographers looking for angles they would not find by hand.
- Anyone who likes to sit back in a beautiful place and simply watch it.
- Couples and groups who want to see themselves together, not the back of their own head.
- Friends who want to watch the same view at the same time, on a tour or at an event.

GOOD TO KNOW
- One small button, a pixel-art clapper in three looks. It stays the same while the camera runs. (Second Life leaves HUDs out of snapshots unless you ask for them.)
- Wear it on a HUD point (Top Right is usual) and allow camera control once.
- It only moves your view, inside the region you are in. Nothing is recorded. When you share, only the camera position goes to the people you invite.
- The Take 12 Watch is free, for friends who do not own Take 12. The Share panel can hand one to a friend near you.
- Read Me notecard included.
- Permissions: (you)
- Support: (you)
```

- **`adboard_vendor_1024.png`** and **`adboard_vendor_512.png`**: a simpler, bolder square for vendor displays, where the picture shows small. Use the 512 for vendors that want a smaller texture.
- The Take 12 Watch has its own board: `watch/marketing/adboard_watch_1024.png`.

The boards use drawn pixel-art "views", **not real screenshots**. Marketplace buyers trust real pictures, so add 5 to 8 real snapshots as extra listing images:
1. A drone view over your favourite region, and a wide horizon at sunrise or sunset (Scenery).
2. A corner view inside a skybox (Scenery, room mode).
3. A group of friends framed together (Us > Only friends).
4. The seated view from above (sit on a bed or a sofa with a friend).
5. Sharing: the Share panel open (hold the button), or the "LIVE - 2 watching" text above the HUD.
6. The status line above the button, for example "Scenery - auto".
7. The menu open on screen: Tap shows the main menu, in the Symbols style.

Tips for the snapshots: use Photo hold for a steady shot, hide your UI (Ctrl+Alt+F1 in Firestorm) except for the shots that show the HUD text, and use a high-quality setting. Keep the same look: square, with a little room around the subject.

The boards say "Your idle time, framed.", "Double-tap to start. Any key brings you back." and a "NEW: SHARE THE VIEW" ribbon. To change a line, edit the `NAME`, `DESCRIPTOR`, `TAGLINE`, `HOWTO` and `NEW` constants (and the chip texts) in `tools/art/make_adboard.py` and run `python3 tools/art/make_adboard.py`. Still is not on the boards on purpose: they sell the automatic camera and sharing. (Lite's boards are paused with Lite; add `--lite` to rebuild them.)
