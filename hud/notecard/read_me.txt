CINEMA HUD  -  READ ME
Your idle time, on film.

Cinema HUD turns Second Life into a quiet film. Your view leaves your avatar and cuts between still, grand views of the place you are in: high drone shots, wide eye-level shots, even the inside of your skybox. Each view is held for about a minute, then it cuts to the next. No effort, no fiddling.


=== QUICK START =========================================

1. Wear the HUD. Right-click it > Attach To HUD > Top Right.
2. Accept the camera permission when asked. (It is needed to move your view. It is only asked once.)
3. DOUBLE-TAP the small clapper button.

That is it. Your camera lifts off and starts showing you the place. Press any movement key, or tap the button once, and your own camera is right back.

You can also just walk away: after a short idle time (90 seconds by default) the scenery camera starts by itself.


=== THE BUTTON ==========================================

The small clapper is the whole HUD. Everything is done with taps.

TAP
   - when idle: opens the menu
   - while rolling: stops (your camera is back)
   - while frozen (Photo): carries on

DOUBLE-TAP  (two quick taps)
   - when idle: starts the scenery camera
   - while rolling or frozen: cuts to the next view, now

HOLD  (about 1 second)
   - when idle: opens the menu
   - while rolling: opens the live menu (see below)

While the camera rolls, the button fades so it stays out of your pictures. It is still there to click. (Change how faint it gets: Options > Fade.)


=== THE FOUR CAMERAS ====================================

SCENERY  (the main one, and the only one that starts by itself)
Looks at the place, not at people. High drone views looking down over the area, and wide views along the horizon, up to about 100 m away, always with a clear view. In a skybox or other closed room in the sky, it stays INSIDE the room: corner views from above, views from the middle, and you as a small figure in the space. On the ground in a house, it looks from outside, above the roof.

PERSONAL
Composed shots of you that follow you as you walk.

SOCIAL
You and the people around you: over-the-shoulder shots when you talk with someone, and group shots when more people join. It cuts to whoever speaks in local chat. When YOU type, it cuts to you, and keeps rolling.

FRIENDS
You and the friends you picked, framed together. While your group stays in the picture, the camera glides gently to keep it there. If someone walks out of the picture for about a second, it cuts to a wider shot that holds everyone.
 - Tap the button > Friends > Pick. Everyone within 20 m is listed. Tap a name to add them. Tap a name with a check mark to take them off again.
 - Friends > Remove lists everyone you saved (even if they are not here), so you can remove just one person.
 - You can save up to 10 friends.

Personal, Social and Friends only start when YOU choose them in the menu.


=== THE LIVE MENU (hold the button while it rolls) =======

PHOTO     Freeze the view so you can take your pictures in peace. Tap once to carry on.
SAVE      Keep the view on screen as a favourite spot. Your saved spots come back now and then. (Scenery only.)
NEXT      Cut to the next view now.
STOP      Give your camera back.
MODES     Switch to another camera.


=== SETTINGS (tap the button > Options, Pace, AFK) =======

PACE      How long each view is held: Lively 30 s, Easy 45 s, Calm 60 s (default), Slow 90 s, Still 120 s.
AFK       When the scenery camera starts by itself: off, 1 min, 90 s, 2 min, 5 min, 10 min, or when Second Life marks you Away.
INDOORS   Auto (default): inside the room when you are in the sky, above the roof when you are in a house on the ground. Or force Inside / Outside.
CHAT CUTS On/Off: Social and Friends cut to whoever speaks.
FADE      How visible the button stays while it rolls: 20, 40 or 70 percent.
ALT-CAM   What happens if you Alt-orbit your camera: pause (default), end, or ignore.
REACH     How far the scenery camera may go. It lowers itself if your viewer holds the camera closer, and Re-learn resets it.
EMOJI     Turn off if your viewer shows boxes instead of little icons on the menu buttons.
TEST CAM  A 3-second check that the HUD can move your camera.
RESET     Back to factory settings.


=== GETTING YOUR CAMERA BACK ============================

Any movement key (Scenery), tapping the button, or starting to type (Scenery and Personal). In Personal, Social and Friends, walking is allowed: the camera follows you. If you Alt-orbit the camera yourself, the HUD pauses until you press ESC.


=== CHAT COMMANDS (optional) =============================

Everything above is in the menu. If you like typing, these work on channel 77 (for example  /77 scenery ).

scenery  personal  social  friends  stop  next  photo  fav  menu  status
pace <seconds>        e.g.  /77 pace 90
reach <metres>        e.g.  /77 reach 60      (or  /77 reach relearn )
indoors auto | inside | outside
friend list | clear
trace on | off        a chat line for every cut
emoji on | off
chan <number>         change the channel
reset

If another HUD already uses channel 77, change it with  /77 chan <number> .


=== QUESTIONS ===========================================

Nothing happens when I double-tap.
Tap once and use the menu to start Scenery. If that works, your double-taps may be too slow: tap twice quickly, about as fast as a double-click. Then type  /77 status  - you should see a line from every part of the HUD. If one is missing, tell the creator.

The menu buttons show boxes.
Options > Emoji > off.

The camera stays close to me.
Some viewers limit how far a script may place the camera. The HUD notices and shortens its distance by itself. If it ever feels too short, Options > Reach > Re-learn.

It does not stay inside my skybox.
Options > Indoors > Inside. (Auto only goes inside when it is sure you are in a closed room in the sky.)

I want to take a picture.
Hold the button, then choose Photo. Tap once to carry on.

Is my chat recorded?
No. In Social and Friends the HUD only notices WHO speaks in local chat, to cut to them. It does not read, store or send anything.

Does it work in a region I do not own?
Yes. The camera stays inside the region you are standing in.


=== GOOD TO KNOW ========================================

 - The HUD needs to be worn on a HUD point (Top Right is the usual one).
 - A recent viewer works best. Very old viewers may show boxes instead of icons, and may limit how far the camera can go.
 - Detach the HUD or take it off any time; your own camera is always restored.

Enjoy the show.
