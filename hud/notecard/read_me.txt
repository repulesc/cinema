TAKE 12  -  READ ME  (8.8.0)
A cinematic camera for Second Life. It only moves your view: nothing is recorded.


=== START ===============================================

1. Wear the HUD on HUD Top Center (any HUD point works) and accept the camera permission once.
2. A short note under the [12] says "Tap the [12] to set up." Tap it: a one-minute setup (each step can be skipped):
   LENS      your viewer's zoom, on one of its own stops: 21 (its wide view), 26, 32, 38 (the default), 47 or 56 mm.
             Ctrl+0 zooms in a stop, Ctrl+8 out, Ctrl+9 back to 21. The page shows your lens live while you zoom.
   EYE LINE  a line on the screen: Page Up / Page Down move it onto your eyes, a tap on the [12] keeps it.
   SIZE      is the strip easy to read? Smaller, Good or Larger.
   THE GUIDE one card: the strip, key by key. Tap the card to close it; Help > Guide (or /12 guide) shows it again.
3. Double-tap the [12]: the strip.

Once it is set up, Take 12 is in AUTO when you wear it: it picks the shot for you (below). Tap a shot, or the ring
at the strip's left end, and you direct yourself.


=== THE [12] ============================================

TAP         the menu: Connect, Settings, Help (setup only)
DOUBLE-TAP  the strip opens or closes (closed, only the [12] stays)
HOLD        everything off (camera, bars, strip); hold again: back exactly as it was

The [12] tells you, quietly:
 a thin ring          a shot is filming
 a small white dot    you are connected with someone
 a soft white glow    someone is watching your film
 amber                something needs you: tap it, the note says what


=== THE STRIP ===========================================

A thin line of words at the top of your screen (Settings > Screen > Place moves it to the bottom; Move in, a step at a time, clears a viewer's taller bars; if they cover the [12] too, type /12 lower):
  ◯  TRACKING  PORTRAIT  FIXED  SCENIC      ▭ 2.39  [12]  ⇄ 2      the running shot's options   the shutter
It stays until you double-tap the [12]. Notes show under the [12], on a dark plate.
The ring at the left end is AUTO; lit, the strip shows only the shot it chose, in faint letters.
The shot that films is underlined; its options are on the right, the ones in use underlined.
The frame key (▭) shows the frame on screen; tap it to choose another, with its snapshot size.
The shutter (a ring with a dot, at the right end) holds the moment for a photo.
The Connect key (two rings) shows who you are with.
Tap the lit shot again and it does its one job (below).


=== AUTO ================================================

AUTO picks the shot from what Take 12 can truly see:
  MOVING                     Tracking, after 1 second of walking or running, from any shot
  STOPPED, ALONE             Tracking holds behind you; after 10 seconds, Scenic: Drift
  STILL 5 MINUTES, OR AWAY   Scenic, wide: Far outdoors, Room indoors
  WITH SOMEONE (within 3 m)  after 4 seconds still together, Portrait: Us, the two of you (or a small group)
  A CROWD                    Scenic: Drift, staying near
  SITTING                    the seat's own view (Fixed), if you framed one; otherwise Drift after 10 seconds
  FLYING                     your own camera until you land
It is calm: a shot stays at least 8 seconds, except Tracking when you move. A key tapped is not a walk.
It never fights you: Alt-click and it waits; press Esc or walk off and it goes on. Its choices are not kept as your
own: your Tracking look, Portrait size and Scenic band stay as you left them.
Tap the faint shot (or /12 tracking, /12 portrait ...) and the camera is yours. Tap the ring (or /12 auto) and AUTO
directs again. Each time you wear Take 12, it starts in AUTO.


=== THE FOUR SHOTS ======================================

TRACKING  a cinematic follow camera.
  SHOULDER · FILM · CLASSIC   over your shoulder; at head height, you on a third (the film); or high and centred (the game)
                              Walking, the frame leaves room ahead of you.
  L · R                       the side of the picture you walk on
  It eases into turns like a steadicam, pulls back when you run, comes in when a wall is behind you, and in a small
  room it takes the room's corner. The lit tap: back behind you.

PORTRAIT  you standing, composed.
  CLOSE · MEDIUM · WIDE       how much of you: to the chest, to the waist, all of you
  ME · US                     you alone, or you and the people within 3 m (up to three of them)
  A soft three-quarter view at eye level, your eyes on the upper third, space in front of your face. Walk and the
  camera comes along; stop and it frames you again. With one friend: a two-shot from the side. The lit tap: the
  other side (with a friend, a second tap crosses to the other side of you both).

FIXED     your own framing, kept.
  Frame the picture with your viewer's camera (Alt + mouse, the wheel) and hold still a second: Take 12 keeps it.
  Standing:  VIEW A · VIEW B, two views for each place. Walk off and the camera comes along, the same angle on you.
  Seated:    each seat keeps its own view, for each spot on it (a sofa's left and right are two views).
             FORGET SEAT forgets this one (UNDO for 3 seconds).
             LET GO: this seat or vehicle's own camera, every time you sit on it (all its copies). TAKE BACK undoes it.
             Sitting down switches to Fixed by itself; standing up gives you back the shot you had.
  A kept view is never replaced by accident: frame a new one and KEEP NEW lights for 5 seconds; tap it to keep.
  The lit tap: back to the kept view.

SCENIC    views of the place. It also starts by itself when you are idle.
  DRIFT · NEAR · ROOM · FAR   a slowly moving camera, views near you, the room you are in, views out to 60 m
  Still frames change about every 15 seconds. Drift glides: close on you, then the camera slides slowly round
you at eye height, then a new close from another side; it stays near when people are around, still in a cabin
(Settings > Motion > Reveal: rising and pulling back to show the place instead).
  Indoors every band stays in the room. The lit tap: the next view now.


=== FRAMES ==============================================

Every frame is a picture. The film has one cinema frame, the same in every shot; tap the ▭ to choose it:
  2.39 · 2.00 · 1.85 · 1.66   bars above and below
  Full                         no bars

THE SHUTTER  press it and the moment holds, exactly as you see it, in your photo frame (bars at the sides).
             Take your snapshot (Ctrl+Shift+S), then press it again: the film goes on.
             While it is open, the ▭ shows your photo frame: 3:2 · 4:3 · 1:1 · 4:5 (3:2 to begin with, one for every
             shot). Alt-click still fine-tunes the view.
The dialogs and the note give the snapshot size for the frame (Snapshot > Custom size).


=== WHAT HAPPENS BY ITSELF ==============================

SITTING        Fixed by itself: the seat's kept view, or frame it. Standing up, the shot you had. (Auto: its own rules.)
ALT-CLICK      your camera, for a look round: the lit shot dims and nothing is shared. A movement key or the lit
               tap gives the shot back.
FLYING, MOUSELOOK   your own camera until you land (or leave mouselook), then the shot comes back.
TELEPORT       the same shot, a fresh picture once the place has loaded.
IDLE           Scenic starts when you are Away, or after 5 minutes with no key and no typing (Settings > Idle start).
               A key or typing gives you back the shot you had.
ZOOM           your lens is your own: zoom and the picture keeps its framing.


=== CONNECT =============================================

Share films with people near you who wear Take 12. Two equal cameras: each of you films your own shot.
- Tap the Connect key (or Menu > Connect): the Connect page. Invite lists the people within 20 m who wear Take 12;
  tap a name and they are asked. Friends (on the same page): add anyone near you; two friends, each in the other's
  list, connect by themselves when near.
  A soft chime when someone asks or joins. While you are alone, the rings count the Take 12s near you.
- With one other person, the Connect key flips between your film and theirs. Menu > Connect lists everyone and
  where they are (filming, watching your film, away); tap a name: Watch, or Show my film.
- The strip says SHARING while someone watches your film, THEIR FILM while you watch theirs.
- Only Take 12's shots are shared: never your own camera, an Alt-click look or mouselook.
- More than 20 m apart, it rests; close again, it comes back by itself. Leave (Menu > Connect) or a hold ends it.
- The free Take 12 Scenery can watch your film too.

(Friends are on the Connect page.)

=== ABOUT ===============================================

Take 12 is made by 12: the maker's name in Second Life, and the name of this camera.
A name ties a thing to one person. A number is broader and quieter, and it points outward: there is something out
there.

In a virtual world what we make can outlast us. A number keeps, without the person behind it.
Two digits, because one stands still and two make movement, in numbers and in living things.
Twelve, because it is how we keep time: the hours on a clock, the months of a year.

A take is a moment in time. Take 12 keeps them.


=== GOOD TO KNOW ========================================

- Feedback: tap the [12], then Feedback (or type /12 feedback). Write a line; it goes straight to the maker by email,
  with your name and Take 12's version.
- The bars are on your screen, not in snapshots. Tap the ▭: its dialog gives the snapshot size for the frame.
- If the strip looks too big or cut off, reset your viewer's HUD zoom (most viewers have it in the View menu).
- The Second Life mobile app shows no HUDs.
- An older Take 12 worn at the same time: take it off. A newer copy worn with this one copies your setup over by itself.
- /12 status prints one line per script, if something seems wrong.
