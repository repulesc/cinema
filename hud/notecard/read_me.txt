TAKE 12  -  READ ME
A new view of Second Life, from beyond your avatar's back.

Take 12 moves your camera off your avatar to the places a camera operator would choose: high drone views over the area, wide views across the horizon, the corners of your skybox, or a frame around you and your friends. It changes the view by itself about once a minute. Any movement key brings your normal camera back. It only moves your view: nothing is recorded.


=== QUICK START =========================================

1. Wear the HUD. Right-click it > Attach To HUD > Top Right.
2. Accept the camera permission when asked. (It is needed to move your view. It is only asked once.)
3. DOUBLE-TAP the small clapper button.

That is it. Your camera lifts off and starts showing you the place. Press any movement key and your own camera is right back.

Or simply stay idle: after 3 minutes with no keys and no mouse (adjustable in the menu) the scenery camera starts by itself. See IDLE START below for the mouse check.


=== THE BUTTON ==========================================

The small clapper is the whole HUD. Everything is done with taps.

TAP  (one click)
   - always a menu: when idle, the menu; while a camera runs, the live menu (see below)

DOUBLE-TAP  (a double-click)
   - when idle: starts the scenery camera
   - while a camera runs: the next view, now

HOLD  (about 1 second, until the clapper claps)
   - a shortcut to the Connect panel (see CONNECT). Connect is in both menus too.

ANY MOVEMENT KEY
   - your own camera, instantly

While a camera runs, the clapper gives a tiny clap now and then; with your own camera it is still. (Its look: Options > Look.)


=== THE CAMERAS ========================================

Five on the menu: SCENERY (the place, while you are away), ME (follows you), US (you and the people around you), STILL (a held picture of you) and FREEHAND (you frame it, it holds the shot). FRAME (see below) sets the picture format on your screen.

THE COMPOSITION. Me, Us and Still place people the way a photographer does, on the golden lines: the eyes 38.2 % from the top of the picture, and you 38.2 % from one side, with the open space on the side you look to. A full-length picture keeps air around you: a little more above the head (10 %) than below the feet (8 %). With a Frame format set, they compose inside that format, not your whole screen.

SCENERY  (the main one, and the only one that starts by itself)
Looks at the place, not at people. High drone views looking down over the area, and wide views along the horizon, up to about 100 m away, always with a clear view. On a build in the sky (a skybox, a sky mall, a sky club), it stays INSIDE: views from up high, at eye level, and you as a small figure in the space, always on your own floor. On the ground in a house, it looks from outside, above the roof (Options > Indoors > Inside keeps it inside there too).

ME
Composed shots of you that follow you as you walk.

US  (tap the button > Us > Everyone near)
You and the people around you: over-the-shoulder shots when you talk with someone, and group shots when more people join. It cuts to whoever speaks in local chat. When YOU type, it cuts to you, and keeps rolling.

In Us, when everyone stands close together, the camera now and then comes in for a close view of your faces, among the wider ones.

US, ONLY FRIENDS  (tap the button > Us > Only friends; Us > Friends list to pick them)
You and the friends you picked, framed together. While your group stays in the picture, the camera glides gently to keep it there. If someone walks out of the picture for about a second, it cuts to a wider shot that holds everyone.
 - Tap the button > Us > Friends list > Pick. Everyone within 20 m is listed. Tap a name to add them. Tap a name with a check mark to take them off again.
 - Friends list > Remove lists everyone you saved (even if they are not here), so you can remove just one person.
 - You can save up to 10 friends.
 - YOUR OWN VIEW (Only friends, needs the lens script): frame it the usual way (Alt-orbit, mouse wheel, or A / D while seated). When you hold still, "Your view is kept · Esc to continue" shows above the button (a notice, not a menu): press ESC and Take 12 carries on with your view - just as close, as high and aimed at the same faces - in every next cut, following your friends if they move.
 - Sitting or lying, W / S step closer or wider.
 - Tap the button > Release to let go of your view and go back to automatic framing (sitting down, standing up or a friend coming or going does it too).

CONNECT
Connect with the people near you, and watch one camera together. Once you are connected, WHOEVER FILMS LEADS: start any camera, and everyone connected sees it. When nobody films, everyone has their own camera, still connected.
 - HOLD the button (or tap > Connect): the Connect panel. It first looks who is near you wearing Take 12 or the free Take 12 Watch.
 - + name connects you with one person, + Everyone with all of them. Someone already connected shows as ⇄ Join name.
 - Friends wearing the Take 12 Watch, or Take 12 with you in their Friends list, are connected at once. Anyone else sees "... wants to connect · double-tap: yes" for 20 seconds (a double-tap or the panel says yes).
 - Connecting starts no camera. Start any camera (or double-tap) and the others see it: "⇄ You film · Ana, Ben see it" above your button. They see "⇄ Pano films".
 - MY TURN: while you watch, start any camera (the menu, a double-tap when nobody films, or Hold > My turn), and you film: everyone, Pano too, now sees yours.
 - A MOVEMENT KEY while you watch gives you your own camera for a moment; stand still for 4 seconds and the shared view is back. Orbit your camera to look around; Esc brings you back.
 - Double-tap while you watch: the next view, for everyone.
 - Everyone sees the same picture, whatever their zoom: each viewer's camera stands where their own lens shows it the same size.
 - Your camera stopping never disconnects anyone: nobody films for a while, that is all.
 - DISCONNECT (Hold > Disconnect, or the key): you leave; the others stay connected. If you were the one holding it, another Take 12 keeps the others connected. If only Take 12 Watches would be left, it asks first ("Ana and Ben will lose the view").
 - Above your button the names show "(own camera)" or "(no signal)"; someone silent for 45 seconds is dropped. Leaving the region or taking the HUD off disconnects you.

STILL
For a picture of yourself, or of you and whoever stands right next to you. Still places the camera well and holds it still: no cuts, and it never moves by itself.
 - Tap the button > Still. DOUBLE-TAP for the next framing: Full length, Half length, Close.
 - Adjust with your movement keys: W / S closer / further, A / D round you. You stand still meanwhile. The next framing starts fresh. PAGE UP / PAGE DOWN raise or lower the camera (FIT, below).
 - SHIFT + A / D (or Shift + the left / right arrows) slides you left or right in the picture, for example to the other golden line, without moving the camera round you.
 - THE LENS: the line above the button names the lens a photographer would use for this framing, and yours:
      □ Still · Close · 105 mm · now 21 · Ctrl+0
   Take 12 can read your viewer's zoom, but only you can change it: Ctrl+0 zooms in, Ctrl+8 zooms out, Ctrl+9 goes back to normal. Press Ctrl+0 until the line shows "105 mm ✓". The camera backs off by itself so the picture keeps its size: a longer lens flattens the perspective, the classic flattering portrait look. Second Life's normal view is about 21 mm, a wide angle.
   The lenses: Full length 50 mm, Half length 85 mm, Close 105 mm. The line shows in Still even when the status line is off.
 - Tap once to leave.

FREEHAND  (tap the button > Freehand)
For the photographer: you frame the shot, of yourself, of people, or of an object; Take 12 holds it.
 - Frame it with Second Life's own camera: Alt-click your subject to aim at it, Ctrl+Alt-drag to orbit around it, Ctrl+Alt+Shift-drag to pan, the mouse wheel to come closer, Ctrl+0 / Ctrl+8 for your lens. Frame (below) shows the picture format.
 - Hold the view still for a second: it is held. The line above the button says "✥ Freehand · held · 85 mm", and the clapper claps.
 - Now it is a tripod: Esc and walking no longer move it. Walk into your picture, pose, take your snapshot.
 - To frame again, just move the camera ("framing · hold still"), and hold still again. Esc always brings back the view last held.
 - The view is aimed at what sits in the middle of your picture. Zoom while it is held, and the camera glides back (or in) so that subject keeps its size: a longer lens flattens the perspective, as everywhere in Take 12. (Phantom objects are seen through: aim at something solid, or frame again after zooming.)
 - Tap > Save keeps the view held (two per region: View A and View B; a third replaces the oldest). Double-tap goes from one saved view to the other; with none saved, it holds the view on screen at once.
 - Stop: tap > Stop. (Movement keys do not stop Freehand: they move you.)

Me, Us, Still and Freehand only start when YOU choose them in the menu.

FIT: PAGE UP / PAGE DOWN  (Me, Us, Only friends, Still)
Second Life plays your pose in your viewer, so no script can see where your body really is: in a deep sofa, on a bar stool, sitting on the ground, in high heels. When the framing is off, show it once, by eye:
 - PAGE UP raises the camera, PAGE DOWN lowers it (or E / C). A tap moves it 5 cm. Hold for more: it starts slowly, for precision, and speeds up to half a metre a second for a big move. (Shift can't help here: Second Life doesn't tell scripts about it.) The camera glides at once, and every next shot is framed with it.
 - Above the button: "Fit +12 cm · this seat".
 - It is kept for this seat (and where on it you sit, so each side of a sofa has its own; the last 20 seats), for sitting on the ground, and for standing. Sit there again and you are framed right from the first shot.
 - Back to automatic: press until it says "Fit 0 · automatic".
 - While a people camera runs, these keys never make you jump; when you fly, they fly.


=== THE KEYS (only if your HUD has the keys strip) ==========

In a few busy moments, a short row of quiet keys appears beside the button. Everything else stays in the menus.
   Connected, holding it    NEXT · INVITE · DISCONNECT    INVITE: connect more people
   Connected, watching      NEXT · MY TURN · DISCONNECT   NEXT cuts for everyone; MY TURN: you film
   Freehand                 SAVE · VIEW A · VIEW B · STOP  the saved view in use is underlined
   Still                    FULL · HALF · CLOSE            the framing in use is underlined
The rest of the time the keys are gone, and the button is alone again.
 - No keys in Freehand or Still? Type  /77 status : the line "[Take 12/keys]" says whether the strip is found. The strip is the one box named "Take 12 keys"; a fifth box linked to the HUD is taken as the strip too, whatever its name, and renamed.


=== FRAME: THE PICTURE FORMAT =============================

Tap the button > Frame, and choose a format: 3:2 (35 mm film), 4:5 (portrait), 1:1 (square), 4:3, 2.39:1 (cinema) or 9:16 (vertical). Your screen outside the picture is dimmed, like a camera's viewfinder, so you still see what is about to come into the frame. Off takes it away.
 - It follows your viewer window: make the window bigger or smaller and the frame follows within two seconds.
 - Me, Us and Still compose inside the format: in 4:5 you stand on a golden line of the 4:5 picture, never in the dimmed part. Still re-frames at once when you change the format; Me and Us from their next shot.
 - Snapshots leave HUDs out, so the dimmed bars are never in your picture. For a picture in the same format, set the snapshot size to the same ratio (for example 1600 x 2000 for 4:5).
 - The dimmed bars cannot be clicked through: a click on them does nothing. Clicks inside the frame work as always.
 - If the frame sits off-centre or is the wrong size, your HUDs may be zoomed: set the HUD zoom back to normal in your viewer's View menu. If the bars appear on the wrong side, type  /77 crop flip ; if they cover the button,  /77 crop back .
 - Chat: /77 crop 4:5 (or any format), /77 crop off.


=== ABOVE THE BUTTON ====================================

While Take 12 films, a short line above the button always says which camera runs and who is in charge:

   ◇ Scenery · auto · 21 mm ✓                you started it (and your lens, see YOUR LENS)
   ◇ Scenery · started while you were idle   the idle start began it
   ○ Me · auto        ◎ Us · auto            □ Still · Close · 105 mm ✓
   ♡ Only friends · auto                     or  · your view  (after you framed it yourself)
   ▣ Photo · held                            (/77 photo) tap > Resume to carry on
   ◇ Scenery · paused · Esc to carry on      you moved the camera yourself

Nothing above the button means: it is your own camera.
While you are connected, "⇄ You film · Ana, Ben see it" or "⇄ Pano films" sits on top of it.
Short messages ("Your view is kept", "Half length", "Ana watches") show for a few seconds, then the line comes back.
Options > Look > Status line turns it off (or /77 statusline off).

The button too small? Options > Look > Size: Small, Medium (the usual) or Large; the keys grow with it (or /77 size l).


=== YOUR LENS ===========================================

Your viewer's zoom is your lens. Take 12 reads it all the time, in every camera, and frames for it. It cannot change it: only you can.
   Ctrl+0   zoom in (a longer lens)
   Ctrl+8   zoom out (a wider lens)
   Ctrl+9   back to normal (about 21 mm, a wide angle)

The rule, as in real photography: the lens changes the perspective, never the size of the picture.
 - Zoom while a camera runs, and the camera glides back (or in) so the people keep their size. A longer lens gives the flatter, more natural faces of a portrait lens; a wider one shows more of the place around them. Every next view is planned for your lens.
 - In Only friends, a view you framed yourself keeps its size through a zoom too.
 - A longer lens also changes WHICH views you get: more close views of faces. A wider one: more of the group and the place.
 - Scenery is about the place: zooming in gives a telephoto detail of the same view.

The line above the button shows your lens, and the range each camera likes:
   ◇ Scenery · auto · 21 mm ✓               Scenery: up to 28 mm
   ◎ Us · auto · 21 mm · try 24 · Ctrl+0     Everyone near: 24 to 50 mm
   ○ Me · auto · 50 mm ✓                     Me: 35 to 50 mm
   ♡ Only friends · auto · 85 mm ✓           Only friends: 35 to 85 mm
"try" is only a suggestion, with the key that gets you there (Ctrl+0 for a longer lens, Ctrl+8 for a wider one): every lens works. Still names one exact lens for each framing (see STILL).


=== TIPS ================================================

While you are new to Take 12, a short tip shows above the button now and then, for a few seconds, under the line that says which camera runs: what you can do next (when the camera starts, when your camera comes back). It never covers the HUD's own messages.
Each tip shows only a few times. After a while Tips turns itself off, and says so once in chat.
Tap the button > Help > Tips to turn them off, or back on (they start again from the beginning).


=== THE LIVE MENU (tap the button while a camera runs) =====

RESUME    Only while the shot is held (/77 photo): carry on.
SAVE      Keep the view on screen as a favourite spot. Your saved spots come back now and then. (Scenery only.)
NEXT      Cut to the next view now.
STOP      Give your camera back.
CONNECT   The Connect panel (as a hold does).
MODES     Switch to another camera.


=== SETTINGS (tap the button > Options) =================

Options has three groups: TIMING (Pace, Idle start), CAMERA (Seated view, Indoors, Reach, Alt-cam, Chat cuts, Phantom) and LOOK (Menu style, Button, Chat text), plus Favourites, Test cam and Reset.


PACE      (Timing) How long each view is held: Lively 30 s, Easy 45 s, Calm 60 s (default), Slow 90 s, Slowest 120 s. Me and Us hold a shot for about three quarters of it, and also wait longer before they cut to a speaker or to someone who arrives.
IDLE START (Timing) When the scenery camera starts by itself: off, 1 min, 90 s, 2 min, 3 min (default), 5 min or 10 min with no keys, no typing and no camera moves.
          MOUSE CHECK (on by default): it also waits until your viewer marks you Away. A script cannot see the mouse, but the viewer can: any mouse movement keeps you from going Away. Set your viewer's own "Away" timer short (in its Preferences, e.g. 2 or 3 minutes), or the scenery camera waits for it. If your viewer never sets you Away, turn the mouse check off.
          Once the scenery camera runs, moving the mouse does not stop it: a movement key, typing in chat, or a tap on the button does.
INDOORS   Auto (default): inside when you stand on a build in the sky, or on a build under a roof (a house, a shop, a trailer, a mall); outdoor views on open land and on roofless decks. Inside: always inside, near you, even where the HUD cannot see the walls. Outside: always outdoor views.
CHAT CUTS On/Off: Us cuts to whoever speaks.
LOOK      MENU STYLE: Symbols (quiet black-and-white marks, the default), Plain (words only) or Color (emoji). BUTTON: Classic, Rec (red light) or Viewfinder (cyan). STATUS LINE (see ABOVE THE BUTTON) and CHAT TEXT are here too.
CHAT TEXT The HUD is quiet in chat by default (only errors, warnings and answers to your commands). Options > Chat text > on gives one line per cut, which explains what it found and why, and a line after each menu choice.
ALT-CAM   What happens if you Alt-orbit your camera: pause (default), end, or ignore.
SEATED    When you sit or lie down, the camera looks down on you from above, in Me and Us: Auto (45 degrees, default), Higher, Lower or Off.
REACH     How far the scenery camera may go (50 / 100 / 150 / 200 m; 100 by default). If your viewer really holds the camera closer, it lowers itself; Re-learn goes back to your choice.
TEST CAM  A 3-second check that the HUD can move your camera.
RESET     Back to factory settings.


=== GETTING YOUR CAMERA BACK ============================

Any movement key (Scenery), tapping the button, or starting to type (Scenery and Me). In Me and Us, walking is allowed: the camera follows you. If you Alt-orbit the camera yourself, the HUD pauses until you press ESC.

=== QUESTIONS ===========================================

Nothing happens when I double-tap.
Tap once and use the menu to start Scenery. If that works, your double-taps may be too slow: tap twice quickly, about as fast as a double-click. Then type  /77 status  - you should see a line from every part of the HUD. If one is missing, tell the creator.

The menu buttons show boxes.
Options > Look > Plain (words only).

The camera stays close to me.
Some viewers limit how far a script may place the camera. The HUD notices and shortens its distance by itself. If it ever feels too short, Options > Reach > Re-learn.

It does not stay inside (a skybox, a mall, a house).
Auto goes inside when you stand on a build that has a roof over you or no land under it for about 100 m. Some builds are made without physics (their floor, walls and roof cannot be seen by the HUD), and Auto cannot know about those. Set Options > Indoors > Inside: it then always stays inside, near you. With Options > Chat text on, the chat line "scenery: ..." says what it found.

I want to take a picture.
Tap the button > Still for a held picture of you, or Freehand to frame it yourself: both hold the camera until you leave.

Is my chat recorded?
No. In Us the HUD only notices WHO speaks in local chat, to cut to them. It does not read, store or send anything.

Does it work in a region I do not own?
Yes. The camera stays inside the region you are standing in.


=== GOOD TO KNOW ========================================

 - The HUD needs to be worn on a HUD point (Top Right is the usual one).
 - A recent viewer works best. Very old viewers may show boxes instead of icons, and may limit how far the camera can go.
 - Detach the HUD or take it off any time; your own camera is always restored.

Enjoy the show.
