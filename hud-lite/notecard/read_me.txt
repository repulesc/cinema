TAKE 12 LITE  -  READ ME
Your idle time, on film.

Take 12 Lite turns Second Life into a quiet film. Your view leaves your avatar and cuts between still, grand views of the place you are in: high drone shots and wide shots along the horizon. Each view is held for about a minute, then it cuts to the next.


=== QUICK START =========================================

1. Wear the HUD. Right-click it > Attach To HUD > Top Right.
2. Accept the camera permission when asked. (Only asked once.)
3. DOUBLE-TAP the small clapper button.

Your camera lifts off and shows you the place. Press any movement key, or tap the button once, and your own camera is right back.

You can also just walk away: after a short idle time (90 seconds by default) the scenery camera starts by itself.


=== THE BUTTON ==========================================

TAP
   - when idle: opens the menu
   - while rolling: stops (your camera is back)
   - while frozen (Photo): carries on

DOUBLE-TAP  (two quick taps)
   - when idle: starts the scenery camera
   - while rolling or frozen: cuts to the next view, now

HOLD  (about 1 second)
   - when idle: opens the menu
   - while rolling: opens the live menu

While the camera rolls, the button fades so it stays out of your pictures. It is still there to click.


=== THE LIVE MENU (hold the button while it rolls) =======

PHOTO     Freeze the view so you can take your pictures in peace. Tap once to carry on.
NEXT      Cut to the next view now.
STOP      Give your camera back.


=== SETTINGS (tap the button > Pace, AFK, Options) ======

PACE      How long each view is held: Lively 30 s, Easy 45 s, Calm 60 s (default), Slow 90 s, Still 120 s.
AFK       When the scenery camera starts by itself: off, 1 min, 90 s, 2 min, 5 min, 10 min, or when Second Life marks you Away.
ALT-CAM   What happens if you Alt-orbit your camera: pause (default), end, or ignore.
REACH     How far the camera may go. It lowers itself if your viewer holds the camera closer.
CHAT TEXT The HUD is quiet in chat by default (only errors and answers to your commands). Options > Chat text > on gives a line for every cut and after menu choices.
EMOJI     Turn off if your viewer shows boxes instead of little icons on the menu buttons.


=== GETTING YOUR CAMERA BACK ============================

Any movement key, tapping the button, or starting to type. If you Alt-orbit the camera yourself, the HUD pauses until you press ESC.


=== THE FULL VERSION ====================================

The buttons with a lock are part of the full Take 12. Tap one and it opens the listing.

 - FRIENDS: you and the friends you picked, framed together, with a gentle glide and a wide cut if someone leaves the picture.
 - SOCIAL and PERSONAL: over-the-shoulder shots, group shots, and shots of you that follow you. They cut to whoever speaks.
 - SKYBOX ROOMS: inside a closed room in the sky, views from the corners and the middle of the room.
 - FAVOURITE SPOTS: save a view you love, and it comes back now and then.
 - BUTTON LOOKS: classic, red record light or cyan viewfinder.


=== QUESTIONS ===========================================

Nothing happens when I double-tap.
Tap once and use the menu to start Scenery. Then tap twice quickly, about as fast as a double-click.

The menu buttons show boxes.
Options > Emoji > off.

The camera stays close to me.
Some viewers limit how far a script may place the camera. The HUD notices and shortens its distance by itself.

Is my chat recorded?
No. This HUD does not listen to chat at all.

Does it work in a region I do not own?
Yes. The camera stays inside the region you are standing in.

Enjoy the show.
